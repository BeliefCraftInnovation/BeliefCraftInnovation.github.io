This Privacy Policy applies to **TennisActionApp** provided by BeliefCraft Innovation. It explains how the current version of the app handles information and sets out our privacy commitments for users and platform review.

## Scope

This policy applies to the current public version of TennisActionApp and related versions distributed through the App Store or other platforms. If future versions change their features or data practices, we will update this policy accordingly.

## How the Current Version Works

The current version of TennisActionApp **does not require account registration**, and users can access the core features without creating an account.

In future versions, we may offer **optional account registration**. If that functionality is introduced, we will update this Privacy Policy before it is enabled to explain what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is handled.

## Information We Currently Collect

In the current version:

- We **do not require registration information**, so we do not collect account-related information such as name, email address, phone number, or similar account identifiers for registration purposes.
- We **do not actively collect** government-issued identification information, contact lists, precise location information, or other sensitive personal information.
- We **do not ask users to upload personal content to our servers** for account creation or user profile building.

## Local Content and Device Permissions

If a user chooses to use videos, photos, or other local content on their device within the app, that content is used only to support the relevant app functionality.

In the current version, based on the intended product design:

- such content is intended to be processed **locally on the device**;
- we **do not proactively upload such content to BeliefCraft Innovation servers**; and
- we do not sell, rent, or use such content to build personally identifiable user profiles.

If a future version introduces cloud sync, account systems, or online processing features, we will update this Privacy Policy before those features are launched and provide additional disclosures where applicable.

## How We Use Information

In the current version, we handle content chosen by the user only to the extent necessary to provide the core app functionality on the device. We do not use such content for advertising, cross-app tracking, or sale to third parties.

## Third-Party Services

In the current version, we **do not track users for advertising purposes**.

If a future version integrates third-party analytics, crash reporting, login, or cloud services that involve information handling, we will update this policy to identify those services and explain their purpose and data practices.

## Children's Privacy

The current version of TennisActionApp is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to us, they may contact us through BeliefCraft Innovation's public channels or the developer contact information shown in the app store listing, and we will address the issue after review.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy based on product changes, legal requirements, or platform review requirements. The updated version will be published on this page and will take effect on the date it is posted.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [support@beliefcraft.cloud](mailto:support@beliefcraft.cloud).
